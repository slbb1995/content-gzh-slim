# Huati body mode

Use only when `writer_mode` is `huati`.

Turn the approved structure into a viewpoint, phenomenon, tension, professional-pressure, emotional-resonance, or discussion article. Keep the approved core judgment central, use only confirmed context for examples or first-person claims, preserve uncertainty, and end with the approved discussion or business connection. Do not turn emotion into invented facts, cases, numbers, or experience.

Apply internal production controls without narrating the writing workflow. Do not explain internal knowledge-base status, missing-material notes or writing instructions to the reader; state the supported viewpoint and necessary uncertainty. Legitimate source attribution and evidence limitations are not private production instructions and must not be hidden.
