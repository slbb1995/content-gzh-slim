# Huati body mode

Use only when `writer_mode` is `huati`.

Develop the approved viewpoint, phenomenon, tension, professional pressure or discussion. For a supplied draft, preserve the effective scenes and sequence instead of resetting it to a template. Use confirmed author facts for first-person experience; attributed external stories can remain external stories with their evidence qualifications. Preserve uncertainty and let the article end naturally when the direction calls for it. Do not turn emotion into invented facts, cases, numbers, or experience.

Apply internal production controls without narrating the writing workflow. Do not explain internal knowledge-base status, missing-material notes or writing instructions to the reader; state the supported viewpoint and necessary uncertainty. Legitimate source attribution and evidence limitations are not private production instructions and must not be hidden.
