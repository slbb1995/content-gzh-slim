# Ganhuo body mode

Use only when `writer_mode` is `ganhuo`.

Turn the approved structure into a practical explanation, process, checklist, SOP, or risk-avoidance article. `ganhuo` controls the value delivered; it does not require a cold, encyclopedic voice and does not suppress the approved IP viewpoint.

Across each major problem unit, complete this reasoning loop at least once: 读者真实处境 → IP 的明确判断 → 专业解释或证据 → 用户可执行的核验动作. A verification action must tell the reader what to do and what to observe, compare, or check. 不要求每个自然段机械重复 this four-part loop; vary paragraph rhythm and let the judgment lead the supporting facts and lists.

Use the approved `voice_mode`, `professional_judgments`, `reader_situations`, and `verification_actions`. Connect to the reader through recognizable situations and explicit trade-offs rather than generic empathy or repeated “我认为”. A professional opinion may use the IP voice, but a first-person experience, client case, result, or number requires a selected confirmed source fragment.

Lead from the reader's concrete problem, make each section perform one approved purpose, distinguish verified facts from missing evidence, and end with the approved small action or business connection. Do not invent steps, results, cases, numbers, or first-person experience.
