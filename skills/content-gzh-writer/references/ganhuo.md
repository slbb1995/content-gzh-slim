# Ganhuo body mode

Use only when `writer_mode` is `ganhuo`.

Turn the approved structure into a practical explanation, process, checklist, SOP, or risk-avoidance article. Lead from the reader's concrete problem, make each section perform one approved purpose, distinguish verified facts from missing evidence, and end with the approved small action or business connection. Do not invent steps, results, cases, numbers, or first-person experience.

Apply internal production controls without narrating the writing workflow. Remove unsupported claims or replace them with a supported general judgment; do not tell the reader that the internal knowledge base lacks a case, that this task can use only stored materials, or that the draft is following writing instructions. Preserve source attribution, evidence limitations and uncertainty needed for the reader to judge the substantive claim.
